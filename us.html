What the data cannot say

Official action vs. final disposition. A case may be alleged, pending, settled, modified or dismissed. Open the original source for the latest procedural status; do not total financial measures across incompatible categories.

FBI IC3 state statistics. The 2025 annual report provides complaint counts, reported losses in USD, and complaint counts per 100,000 population for the 50 states and D.C. IC3 complaints are not independently confirmed crimes; population-normalized rates still reflect reporting behavior and coverage. Territorial figures are in the original report but not plotted here.

State reference vs. incident location. A state named in a headline is a research clue. A marker is placed at an approximate state-capital reference point, not an event address, defendant headquarters or geographic rate.

Uneven news coverage. State searches are rotated, so zero displayed items never means zero fraud. Syndicated titles are not independently verified incidents.

Agency scope. Automated official index collection initially covers FinCEN and CFPB; SEC, DOJ, FTC, OCC, FDIC, FBI IC3, state attorneys general and local police require additional source-specific collection and quality review.
