# Contributing to Signal North

Signal North collects publicly sourced **Canadian fraud, scams, crypto, gaming and AML enforcement intelligence**. Community links should help researchers understand what original publications actually establish.

## Two supported public routes

- [Suggest a public source](https://github.com/khaled-agi/Signal-North/issues/new?template=public-source.yml)
- [Suggest a correction](https://github.com/khaled-agi/Signal-North/issues/new?template=correction.yml)

These routes **require a GitHub account and are not anonymous**. Anything posted there may be public and indexed. Community contributions **do not appear automatically** in the site's official article index or on the map. All links require editorial review.

## What we want

Canadian articles, police releases, regulator decisions, official statistics, primary reports and source-linked research on fraud and AML; evidence about casinos and iGaming, payment providers, banks, crypto and securities platforms; updates on appeals, penalties, refunds, recoveries and prosecutions; public social-media posts *as unverified pointers* accompanied by corroborating public original reporting whenever possible.

## Do not submit

Personal identifiers, financial-account or credit-card numbers, SINs, passwords, private messages, private phone numbers, home addresses, screenshots of non-public accounts, customer files, leaked datasets, confidential AML investigations, allegations against identifiable private people, or personal anecdotes containing details that could identify an individual.

Don't post unverified accusations naming a person or company as a proven fraudster. Distinguish an **impersonated brand**, a **customer's bank**, a **regulatory subject** and a **proven liable party**. Preserve the source's procedural or appeal status.

If you experienced a fraud, contact your bank, the police and the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/report-signalez-eng.htm). Signal North is **not** a law-enforcement reporting service, emergency service or legal advisor.

## Moderation

Maintainers may decline, redact or remove material that is personal, defamatory, irrelevant, misleading, duplicative, unsourced, copyrighted, spam or otherwise unsafe. A credible original source link does not guarantee publication. Accepted information must retain attribution, dates, outcome/status and evidence tier. A moderator should review sensitive content before any import.

Do not automatically publish issue submissions or run arbitrary submitted URLs as code. The GitHub issue queue is for editors to inspect manually.

## Anonymous submissions — not enabled yet

GitHub Issues disclose an account username. Static GitHub Pages cannot by itself provide confidential intake, abuse controls or guaranteed anonymity. Before launching a genuinely anonymous option, the project requires separate secure server-side intake, an explicit privacy/retention/deletion policy, restrictions on attachments, automated anti-spam, access controls, rate limits, human moderation and safeguards against malicious accusations. Even then, no internet-based channel should claim absolute anonymity.

## Social sources

Links to public social-media discussions may suggest emerging scam patterns but are **not verified cases**. Do not scrape Reddit or other services without applicable permission or API terms; don't copy usernames, profiles, deleted posts or private discussions into the dataset. Reddit currently requires approval for its API and separate permission for commercial use.

## Data model

Keep article publication date distinct from alleged incident date. Label customer-reported loss, amount recovered, credit exposure, bank-incurred losses and regulatory penalties as different financial-impact types. Repeated reporting of one event should not be summed as multiple losses.
